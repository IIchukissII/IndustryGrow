Applies to third-party EDA library content obtained from SnapEDA
(https://www.snapeda.com/parts/AS7331-AQFT/ams/view-part/) for the ams OSRAM
AS7331 and retained in this repository:

  store/industrygrow.pretty/PQFN50P365X260X119-16N.kicad_mod
  store/industrygrow.kicad_sym
  store/industrygrow.3dshapes/AS7331.step

The delivered package (AS7331-AQFT.zip) carries no licence text, so the terms
under which it is supplied are unstated here. No licence grant is asserted or
implied by its presence in this repository, and it is not covered by this
project's own licences. All rights remain with SnapEDA and, for the 3D model,
with its upstream author, which may be the manufacturer rather than SnapEDA.

The footprint and symbol have been modified from the delivered versions: the
pads were given a solder-mask layer and an explicit mask expansion in place of
16 unfilled mask polygons, a 3D model reference was added, the footprint's
silkscreen reference text was raised from 0.787402 mm to 1 mm to meet the board
minimum text height, the symbol was renamed AS7331-AQFT to AS7331, its stacked
VSSA pins were un-hidden so they no longer form an implicit global net, and
SnapEDA's price and availability properties were removed.

store/industrygrow.kicad_sym is a project symbol library that today holds only
this vendor-derived symbol. When the first project-authored symbol is added to
it, its annotation in REUSE.toml must be revisited: the file will then be of
mixed provenance and this blanket attribution will no longer be correct.
