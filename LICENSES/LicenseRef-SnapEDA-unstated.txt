Applies to third-party EDA library content obtained from SnapEDA
(https://www.snapeda.com/parts/AS7331-AQFT/ams/view-part/) for the ams OSRAM
AS7331 and retained in this repository:

  store/industrygrow.pretty/PQFN50P365X260X119-16N.kicad_mod
  store/industrygrow.3dshapes/AS7331.step

The delivered package (AS7331-AQFT.zip) carries no licence text, so the terms
under which it is supplied are unstated here. No licence grant is asserted or
implied by its presence in this repository, and it is not covered by this
project's own licences. All rights remain with SnapEDA and, for the 3D model,
with its upstream author, which may be the manufacturer rather than SnapEDA.

The footprint has been modified from the delivered version: the pads were given
a solder-mask layer and an explicit mask expansion in place of 16 unfilled mask
polygons, a 3D model reference was added, and the silkscreen reference text was
raised from 0.787402 mm to 1 mm to meet the board minimum text height.

The delivered symbol was also retained and modified for a time - renamed
AS7331-AQFT to AS7331, its stacked VSSA pins un-hidden so they no longer formed
an implicit global net, and SnapEDA's price and availability properties removed.
It was removed from store/industrygrow.kicad_sym on 2026-08-23, when ADR-0014
rev 6 withdrew the AS7331 from M02-LIGHT. That library holds project-authored
symbols only and is no longer covered by this attribution. The footprint and 3D
model above are retained while the E0003 layout still references the footprint.
